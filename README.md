## ✨ Features

- 🕒 **Smart Time Suggestions**: Automatically finds overlapping work hours across time zones  
- 🌍 **Full Timezone Support**: Powered by `Intl` API (100% browser-native)  
- 📅 **Custom Work Days & Hours**: Define availability per member (e.g., Mon–Fri, 09:00–17:00)  
- 🎨 **Interactive Heatmap**: Visualize common availability with intuitive color coding  
- ⏱️ **Flexible Meeting Durations**: 30m, 1h, 2h — only valid slots are shown  
- 📤 **Shareable Links**: Collaborate via unique URL — no sign-up needed  
- ✏️ **Edit & Delete Members**: Fully interactive team management  
- 🌓 **DST-Aware**: Warns when daylight saving affects a member’s schedule  
- 📱 **Fully Responsive**: Works flawlessly on mobile, tablet, and desktop  
- 🔒 **Offline-First**: Runs entirely in your browser — works without internet

---

## 🚀 How to Use

1. Go to the [Live Demo](https://ehsanmns.github.io/timesync-pro)
2. **Add team members** with their name, timezone, work days, and hours
3. Click **"Find Common Slots"**
4. Share the generated link with your team — anyone can view or edit!

> 💡 Tip: The app auto-saves your session in `localStorage`. Refresh? No problem!
